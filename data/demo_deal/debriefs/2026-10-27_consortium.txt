Debrief of Tuesday's meeting with the Nordlink Bay consortium, Port-Lazen – Kerlis cable project.

Well, a rather positive meeting overall. Julien Marec, the CTO of Baltis Telecom, is clearly on our side: he said our repeater solution was "the most robust technically". On the other hand their CFO, Sophie Lenoir, insisted a lot on the payment schedule, she wants to spread it over four years instead of three, otherwise she won't sign off.

On financing, the Atlantic Development Bank is still waiting for the environmental impact study before committing. Thomas Riou, their relationship manager, wasn't there, we haven't seen him for six weeks now. I think we need to chase him.

The competitor, Oceanix Cables, was mentioned twice, apparently they offered a lower price, but I'm not sure of the amount, maybe ten percent less.

Next steps: send a four-year payment schedule proposal before the 15th, and set up a meeting with the bank before the end of the month.
